Who you work with

You talk to the person who builds it

Purplebox is led by a software engineer with five years of building production systems, most recently working inside client teams as a forward-deployed engineer.

There is no sales team and no hand-off to someone you have never met. The person on your first call is the person who designs your system and answers your WhatsApp after launch.

We are a private limited company registered in Hyderabad and work with businesses across India.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell us how your business runs and we handle the rest, including the domain, hosting, email and payment gateway setup. Everything is registered in your name.

How long does it take?

It depends on what you need. A business website is a matter of weeks, and a system with booking, billing or an app takes longer. The quote gives you a delivery date, and you see progress every week until then.

Who owns the website and the data?

You do. The code, the domain, the cloud account and all customer data belong to your business. If you ever move to another developer, we hand everything over.

What if I stop the monthly plan?

Give 30 days' notice and we pass you the code, the data and the logins. You can host it yourself or with someone else. Nothing is switched off to force you to stay.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Usually, yes. We look at what you have on the free call and tell you plainly whether it is better to fix it or rebuild it.

Can it work with what I use now?

In most cases. We regularly connect to payment gateways, WhatsApp, SMS providers, Tally exports, Google Sheets and Microsoft 365. If your current software can export data, we can usually work with it.
