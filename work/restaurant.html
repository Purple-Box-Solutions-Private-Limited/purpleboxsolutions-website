The house

A short menu, cooked the slow way.

Kewra House opened in 2019 in an old house on Road No. 36, with a courtyard, a neem tree and a head cook who had spent thirty years making biryani for weddings.

The menu is short on purpose. Every dish is one a Hyderabadi grandmother would recognise, made with sealed pots, a granite stone and wood coals. Nothing is finished in a microwave.
